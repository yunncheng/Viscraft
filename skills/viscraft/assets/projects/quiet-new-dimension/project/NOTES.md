# Continue this campaign

The Project manifest owns the edit, selected video Assets, image target and mix.
The complete case README explains the production choices and normal commands.

- The original sheet is `assets/product-reference.png`; preserve its product
  silhouette and small features when changing the campaign.
- The launch poster and closing frame use editable text and original image
  pixels inside authored cutout contours. They are not newly generated product
  photography, a rig or a reconstructed 3D model.
- All 192 declared JPEGs drive the sound-space compositor deterministically.
  Its small central mask and exact-product overlay are local creative code,
  not a general background-removal or object-replacement feature.
- Edit the score with its native `samples` render contract. At the included
  settings the programme is 1,440,000 samples, 48 kHz stereo.
- All picture placement audio is muted in this particular mix. The included
  H3 files retain native sound for evaluation or a different mix.
- Replacing generated material is a new authorized operation. The archived
  requests describe what was actually submitted; they do not configure a new
  service or grant spending/upload authorization.
- For hand adjustment, Save/autosave write the current design. Export image
  saves, renders, updates a bound delivery and downloads. Undo and both resets
  use the current browser editing session; reopening starts from the latest save.
