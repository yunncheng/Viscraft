# Continue Five More Minutes — 再等五分钟

A procrastinating cloud sees a flower with an empty cup, decides to release one
drop, and discovers that starting is enough. The complete story is 60 s at 24 fps
(1,440 frames), natively rendered at 1920×1080. `film.js` scales its logical
staging and redraws paths, type and paper at capture size. Sound is 48 kHz stereo.
Eleven picture Sources and one
sound Source are real browser outputs, with no provider, upload or model cost.

Keep the asymmetrical cream cloud, coral scarf, dusty-blue wash, pencil outline,
paper grain and flower/cup relationship coherent. Drawing details use a 12 Hz
clock; camera and some local motion remain continuous. The captions and speech
bubbles are authored into the picture, not editable subtitle tracks. The local
Noto Sans SC variable font and its complete OFL licence must travel with the work.

Shot starts in seconds: 0, 5, 11, 15, 19, 25, 31, 36, 40, 44, 53; end 60.
`project.json` is the authority for durations, selection and shot intent.
`author/film.js` owns drawing and camera. `author/courage.html` loads `film.js`
then `courage.js` to specialize only the 36–40 s hold/release shot: fingers must
meet the actual raindrop before the hands open. That visible contact was repaired
after reviewing the first cut. Do not drop this Source's extra resource.

`author/score.js` renders music and Foley together. One six-note motif changes
orchestration through hesitation, fear, tenderness, courage and the rain waltz.
The intended music rest is 39.3–41.62 s, the first water/chime event is 41.62 s,
the rain waltz begins at 44 s, and the cadence/tail finishes within 60 s.
Pluck, bell, flute/reed-like voices, alarm, paper, breath and rain are procedural
sound, not real instrument/field recordings. Music/Foley have internal gains but
the Project has one combined sound Source/bus, not independent recorded stems.

The authored mastering targets are -17 LUFS / -2 dBTP. Check actual loudness
and true peak in the review of each delivered render rather than treating the
targets as measured compliance. The source has 2,880,000 samples/channel and no tail truncation.
No subjective listening or full-motion review is part of the record; measure and
observe new renders on their own merits. Designed holds, the redraw clock and the
fixed paper can trigger a coarse repeated-frame detector; its counts do not prove
exact duplicate frames, fluid motion or a defect.

After edits: use the local Runtime to plan; preview the risky passage and its
neighbors; build; review; then `deliver . --movie --review`. Unchanged Source
inputs should reuse their Takes. The original local repair changed `cloud_08`
while keeping the other ten picture Sources and soundtrack available for reuse.
An initial reproduction has no original Takes to reuse and must render first.
Do not edit the reference installation or hand-delete Runtime-owned data.

Copy the whole case and continue in its project/ directory. The current manifest
registers the font Asset and the native sample clock. It carries no active Result,
paid jobs, generation credentials or original Build history; building creates
new execution records. The original complete production remains with its owner.
See LICENSES.md and assets/fonts/OFL-NotoSansSC.txt before redistributing material.
