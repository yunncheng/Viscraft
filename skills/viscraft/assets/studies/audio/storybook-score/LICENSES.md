Attribution for the material actually adopted in this study
===========================================================

Upstream: lemo-opuscar, commit e3cb16f63768c68693ff0e15171d6e755457cc32
(https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar), Copyright (c) 2026 LemoLab,
MIT. The full MIT text above applies to the adopted material listed here.

Adopted from `styles/crayon-book/demo/music/score.py` (the musical shape and
selected synthesis mechanisms, not the whole score):

- `THEME` (lines 68–78): the four-bar F | Bb | C7 | F phrase, its pitch order,
  beat positions and durations, used as the recognizable theme in the opening
  and the complete theme section. The Viscraft study transposes and re-orchestrates
  it, adds its own counter-lines, bass, answer phrases and a different 40 s form.
- The toy/reed change idea (lines 94–119): toy piano on the off-beats, reed
  staccato down-beats, turning accents, a rising answer and a reed wind-down.
  The actual notes, timings and alternate answer here are original.
- The breath/wind-down gesture (lines 145, 183–194) and the silent rest
  (lines 119, 225–228): a rest with no new attacks and a gated silence, plus a
  short wind-down before it.
- The winding-down coda idea (lines 196–211): playback rate 1 -> 0.93 applied to
  a rendered coda buffer so onsets and pitch slow together. The Viscraft coda
  buffer, phrase, rate curve integration and measured event mapping are original.
- `toy()` (lines 30–44): the toy-piano inharmonic partial ratios 1, 2.92, 5.84,
  9.3, 1.004, hammer noise, wooden thud, low-pass and level normalisation, adapted
  to JavaScript in `synth.mjs` `toyPiano` with a seeded detune and phases.

Adopted from `core/audio/pluck.py`:

- `_modal` `music_box` branch (lines 191–210): partial ratios 1, 1.003, 6.27,
  17.55, 3, per-partial decays, mechanical click, comb body resonances and the
  duration release, adapted to JavaScript in `synth.mjs` `musicBox`.
- The `harp` preset (line 99) and the string model concepts (fractional delay,
  one-pole loop loss, frequency-dependent t60, `_excite`), adapted to a compact
  Karplus-Strong loop in `synth.mjs` `harp`. The Viscraft implementation uses a
  linear fractional read, a fixed one-pole damping coefficient and its own
  excitation/envelope, and is not a line-for-line port of the numba kernel.

Not adopted: the upstream whole-film modules, `events.json` glints, sampled
flute/clarinet/glockenspiel, `sampler.py`, `sfx.py` mixing helpers and the
upstream `S.room` reverb. The flute and reed are original additive/formant
synthesis for this study. The Schroeder room in `synth.mjs` follows the small
deterministic room pattern used by Viscraft's other score studies and is
not a claim about a real acoustic space.

Everything else in this directory (the 40 s form, section/stem dynamics, the
alternate variant, its answer phrases, cues and orchestration, the renderer,
create recipe, README and sound page) is original Viscraft work.
