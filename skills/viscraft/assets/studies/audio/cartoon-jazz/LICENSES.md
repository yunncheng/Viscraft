This reference contains **no recordings and no sample library**. Every sound is
generated at render time by `synth.mjs`; the arrangement is data in `score.mjs`.
The only borrowed material is MIT-licensed **code technique**, adapted into
JavaScript and credited below. No CC BY drum kit, no CC0 sample pack and no TTS
is used or required.

## Adapted code — lemo-opuscar (MIT)

- Source: `lemo-opuscar`, author LemoLab, commit
  `e3cb16f63768c68693ff0e15171d6e755457cc32`.
- Copyright © LemoLab.
- Adapted files:
  - `styles/rubber-hose/demo/music/score.py` — the syncopated F-major theme and
    its stride/oompah accompaniment, the F harmonic plan, the slide-whistle and
    spring-boing gestures, the stop-time gate idea and the small synthetic room.
  - `core/audio/pluck.py` — the Karplus-Strong waveguide and plucked-string body
    resonance used by the banjo voice.
  - `core/audio/sfx.py` — equal-power pan, one-pole filtering, band-limited
    noise and the authoring-headroom/limiting practice.
- Modifications:
  - Rewritten as dependency-free ES modules for the current
    `window.viscraftSound` contract; no Python, NumPy, SciPy, numba, SoXR or
    external package is imported at render time.
  - The score is re-authored as a 32-second, 120 BPM, 16-bar data passage on the
    24 fps / 48 kHz clock. The seven sections, the base/alternate variants, the
    phrase choices and the orchestration are authored for this reference.
  - The upstream sampler and all recorded instruments are **not** used. Every
    voice here is oscillator/filter/noise synthesis, and the files say so.
  - Stride piano, tuba, banjo, trumpet, muted trumpet, trombone, clarinet,
    xylophone, brushes, snare, bass drum, cymbal, tick, woodblock, boing and
    slide whistle are re-created as small programmatic voices; no recorded
    instrument is imitated by relabelling a sample.
  - The rest is applied to the music bus **after** its room send, while the
    foley bus stays dry: this is the reference's subject, not an upstream
    behaviour copied verbatim.
  - No upstream scenes, story, film, fonts, voice, TTS installer, mux or second
    timeline are present.

## No sample or library licences

Unlike a sampled-score reference, this directory carries no WAV files and no
`SAMPLE_PACK`. If a future edit adds a recording, that recording needs its own
provenance and licence text; the MIT text above does not cover it. Do not add a
CC BY drum kit or a TTS voice without recording the exact source, version and
attribution in this file and in the Project's `LICENSES.md`.
