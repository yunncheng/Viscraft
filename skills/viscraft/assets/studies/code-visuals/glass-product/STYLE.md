# Glass and metal product-lighting study

Use geometry, materials, lighting and viewpoint together: a legible silhouette,
controlled glass transmission/reflection, metal highlights, a grounded stage and
camera movement that reveals construction. Bloom is a finishing choice; it does
not replace believable material or hide incomplete product geometry.

The [Three.js implementation and commands](README.md) supply one specific
dark-studio lens-glass study and its studio/camera beats. It is not a general
product-photography service and does not inherit a commercial or photographic
quality claim from the upstream demo. Its existing product and sequence are
editable source, not an obligation to use that object or an exploded view.
Change geometry, material, light and coverage to fit the actual product; use
suitable imported or generated material when that is the better route to the
required result.

Reuse is by author code: `create.mjs`, `glass-product.mjs`,
`glass-product-scene.html`, `three-files.mjs` and the pinned three.js closure
ship in this directory, and the recipe copies the pinned closure with its
destinations and hashes. Changing proportions, palette or one camera/lighting
beat is the intended adaptation path; a small render checks that the change is
visible, not photographic or commercial acceptance. Dependencies and retained
licence texts are described in the implementation README.
