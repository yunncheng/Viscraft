# Layered watercolor material

Combine readable flat shapes and deliberate ink contours with translucent
washes, wet edges, pigment pooling and paper grain. The colour masses and their
overlap should communicate depth before texture is added. Progressive painting
can reveal a scene; completed marks keep their positions as time advances.

This is a local layered-material reference, not a complete watercolour style, a
painter for arbitrary images, or a claim that the grove and meadow are finished
illustrations. [The implementation and commands](README.md) show the material
process on those bounded subjects. Adapt subject geometry, wash behaviour,
palette and framing in the author code. Reuse its material process without
inheriting its landscape or reveal sequence.

Reuse is by author code: `create.mjs`, `cel-watercolor.mjs`,
`cel-watercolor-scene.html` and `rights.mjs` ship in this directory, and the
recipe declares the module as a Source resource. Putting the same ordered paint
stack under different geometry, text or chart layout is the intended adaptation
path. That is a mechanism study, not a finished film or a gallery-quality
guarantee.
