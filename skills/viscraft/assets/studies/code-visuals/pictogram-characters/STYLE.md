# Style — pictogram characters

## Intent

A flat, geometric pictogram world where the icon **is** the character. The
silhouette carries identity; limbs are simple rounded capsules that grow out of
the icon's own points or lobes. The scene should read as a diagram that can act,
not as a person wearing an icon mask.

## Rules of the look

- **Body first.** Choose one icon silhouette (star, cloud, gear). Arms attach
  inside the side lobe (cloud) or at the side point (star, gear); legs attach at
  two lower outline points. Never add a neck, head, torso or shoulders around it.
- **Two values per actor.** The body and near limb use the actor colour; the far
  limb uses a darker shade of the same colour. No outline, no gradient, no
  facial features.
- **Flat stage.** Warm paper field, one ground line, one soft elliptical shadow
  per actor. No official event grid, motif or emblem language.
- **One prop at a time.** A disc or baton is enough. Its carrier ring changes
  colour at the transfer; the object itself keeps its material colour.
- **Depth explains the action.** The prop is in front of the bodies and behind
  the near hands. Do not draw it over the hands.
- **Timing is event-based.** Prepare, contact, transfer, result hold. The result
  must live inside the authored frames; do not append an unrendered tail.

## Palette starting points

| Role | Variant A | Variant B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Field | `#f3eee2` | `#f1ece1` |
| Ground | `#ddd2bd` | `#d9cdb8` |
| Ink / line | `#17212b` / `#b6a98f` | `#161f2b` / `#b3a58b` |
| Sender body | `#d9543b` | `#6c5ba8` |
| Receiver body | `#2f6f8f` | `#c9792e` |
| Prop | `#e0a33a` | `#2b8c86` |

These are starting points, not a brand palette. Replace them freely; keep the
contrast between the two actors and the prop.

## Avoid

- An icon used as a head on a human body, a round-head skeleton, or a stick
  figure with an emblem floating above it.
- The official event name, marks, dates, slogan, five-colour identity, motif or
  pattern language, and the upstream `ref/` images.
- Whole-frame jitter, noise or motion blur standing in for contact; contact is a
  short ring/pulse plus a change of ownership, not a texture.
- Adding a music bed or title font to make the study feel finished.
