# Painted and cut-paper materials

Three related starting points live in the [implementation](README.md), each with
its own entry Source in the same directory: `wash` (`wash-panel`) for watercolor
washes with deposited edges; `brush` (`brush-panel`) for width-profiled ink
strokes with broken bristles; and `collage` (`collage-panel`) for layered
torn-paper shapes with thickness/shadow cues. Choose the mechanism that serves
the subject and combine it with the work's own shapes and movement. They are
three readings of one material study, not three packages.
Paper grain stays anchored; optional line boiling is an intentional animation
choice, not uncontrolled frame-to-frame noise.

The supplied landscape and still-life scenes exercise these materials. They do
not repaint an arbitrary bitmap, and their simple subjects are not a quality
target for a commissioned illustration. Adapt the drawing, scale, palette and
timing; judge the result against the brief.
